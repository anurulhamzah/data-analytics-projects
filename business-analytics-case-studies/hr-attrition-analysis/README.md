# HR Attrition Analysis

Analysis of employee data to find which factors a company should address to reduce its attrition rate from 15% to 10% within 12 months.

**Tools and methods:** Spreadsheet, issue tree, metrics, HR analytics, Tableau dashboard

## Business context
A data analyst at a company (XYZ Ltd.) is asked to describe employee data and provide insight so that management can apply HR strategies that make employees more productive. Management wants to know which variables matter most and need to be addressed first to reduce attrition.

## Objective
Identify the most important factors behind attrition and prioritise actions to bring the rate down from 15% to 10% in 12 months.
