# Passenger Satisfaction Analysis

Analysis of passenger questionnaire data for a young airline, covering satisfaction drivers and passenger segmentation, to guide service improvements and partnerships.

**Tools and methods:** Spreadsheet, XLMiner Analysis ToolPak, descriptive statistics, EDA, correlation, regression, chi-square, cluster analysis, logistic regression, random forest, Tableau dashboard

**Team:** this was a team project (Team J, four people). My role was project manager.

## Situation
Wings Airline has operated for one year and plans to grow through partnerships with travel agents and new features. The Director of Services and Commercial asked the data analyst team for insight to support these goals.

## Objectives
- Assess how the airline performs on the touchpoints available to passengers.
- Identify the touchpoints that matter most for improving satisfaction.
- Cluster passengers to identify the target market, so management can choose suitable additional features.

## Methodology
1. Data preparation
2. Descriptive statistics
3. Exploratory data analysis
4. Correlation, regression, chi-square
5. Cluster analysis
6. Machine learning

## Key insights
- Overall passenger satisfaction is 43%.
- Satisfaction is lowest in Economy class.
- Most dissatisfied passengers fly short-haul routes.
- Touchpoints to improve: online services and gate location (pre-flight), cleanliness, Wi-Fi, food and drink (in-flight), and departure/arrival time convenience (post-flight).
- Four passenger clusters were found. Based on a Pareto chart, "Traveling Families" and "The Executives" are the priority customers for marketing.

## Recommendations
- Partner with online travel agents to reduce server maintenance cost and add value to online services.
- Provide golf cart support to help passengers reach their gates.
- Introduce guest service procedures for friendly and fast service.
- Wi-Fi: change the internet provider in Business class for a better connection; offer reasonably priced Wi-Fi in Economy and Economy+.
- Offer more content, such as free music and games and reasonably priced movie rentals.
- Offer more food and drink choices, including vegetarian and vegan options.
- Introduce cabin cleaning procedures and best practices for the crew.
- Future analysis: add accounting data (ticket pricing and revenue) and IT data (website visits and booking time per passenger).
