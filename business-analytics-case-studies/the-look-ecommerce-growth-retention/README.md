# The Look Ecommerce: Growth Analysis and Retention Rate

SQL analysis to find product categories with the lowest business growth (revenue and profit) over the past year and to understand current customer retention, in order to drive new retention initiatives.

**Tools and methods:** SQL, growth analysis, retention rate, cohort analysis

## Business context
The Look is a fictitious clothing e-commerce site created by the Looker team. Its synthetic dataset covers customers, products, orders, logistics, web events, and marketing campaigns. The scenario: a data analyst on the analytics team supports product, business, and marketing stakeholders while the company is in optimisation mode because of a potential 2023 crisis. Management plans to cut resources from the lowest-growth categories and wants to understand and improve user retention.

## Objectives
- Find the categories with the lowest revenue and profit growth in the past year, to optimise category resources.
- Understand current retention performance and propose initiatives to raise it.
- Make recommendations.

## Key insights
- Jumpsuits & Rompers had the lowest sales growth this year (a 71% increase over the previous year) and one of the lowest profit increases (revenue up 75%).
- Pants & Capris and Leggings had the biggest average monthly growth in 2022.
- Some categories have negative average monthly growth.
- New users grew every month in 2022, but overall retention is only about 3%: most new customers do not buy again the following month.

## Recommendations
- Diversify Jumpsuits & Rompers after market research, with new designs and styles that match current trends.
- Collaborate with influencers or designers on exclusive collections, and review pricing for the category.
- Invest further in Pants & Capris, with more designs, styles, and sizes.
- Analyse categories with negative monthly growth to understand the cause (customer preferences, saturation, competition).
- Focus on retention with personalised email campaigns, targeted promotions, and loyalty programs.
